### Assistant

**Astronomy interest on Ukrainian Wikipedia is declining, not growing.** Here's what the data shows:

## What was measured
The Wikipedia article "Астрономія" (astronomy), comparing the last 12 complete months (September 2025–August 2026) with the same months a year earlier (September 2024–August 2025).

## Results
"Article views fell 59.6% (16,614 -> 6,708 views in total over the 12 compared months). The whole uk Wikipedia shrank 24.6% over the same time, so the article's share of all views fell 46.4%: it lost ground relative to the rest of that Wikipedia. Verdict: declining, with medium confidence (probably real, but weakened by the reasons listed)."

The article averages 559 monthly views currently.

## How much can you trust this?
**Medium confidence** — probably real, but with caveats. The reasons:
- About 559 views a month is a small base
- The share fell 46.4%, more than the ±26% that month-to-month variation could produce
- The trend holds for typical months, not just one spike
- 10 of 12 months were below the same month a year earlier

The analysis already accounts for seasonality by comparing the same calendar months a year apart, so that's not a weakness here.

## What this means for your course decision
Pageviews measure reader curiosity on Wikipedia, not willingness to pay or course demand—use this to choose what to validate next, not as a standalone decision. The declining trend suggests Ukrainian Wikipedia readers' interest in astronomy is going down, so you'd want to validate demand through other channels (surveys, market research, competitor analysis) before committing to an astronomy course for that audience.
