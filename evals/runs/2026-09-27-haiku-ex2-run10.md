### Assistant

Interest in astronomy is **declining** on Ukrainian Wikipedia, not growing—and you can trust that decline with medium confidence.

## What was measured

The article "Астрономія" (Astronomy) on Ukrainian Wikipedia, comparing the most recent 12 complete months (September 2025–August 2026) with the same months a year earlier.

## Result for Ukrainian Wikipedia

**Article views fell 59.6% (16,614 → 6,708 views in total over the 12 compared months). The whole uk Wikipedia shrank 24.6% over the same time, so the article's share of all views fell 46.4%: it lost ground relative to the rest of that Wikipedia. Verdict: declining, with medium confidence (probably real, but weakened by the reasons listed).**

At about 559 views per month recently, the astronomy article draws modest traffic.

## How far to trust this decline

The verdict is **probably real**, but with clear limits. The reasons are:

- **about 559 views a month is a small base** — low-traffic articles are noisier.
- **the share fell 46.4%, more than the ±26% that month-to-month variation could produce** — the decline is larger than normal noise.
- **the trend holds for typical months, not just the spike in 2024-09** — it's consistent, not driven by a single anomaly.
- **10 of 12 months were below the same month a year earlier** — the direction is stable.

In short: the decline is probably real, but the small monthly volume means there's genuine uncertainty. If you're designing for Ukrainian learners, this suggests astronomy is losing mindshare on their Wikipedia—but validate that with other signals (course enrollments, search trends, educator demand).

**Scope**: Pageviews measure reader interest on Wikipedia, not willingness to pay or market size—use this to choose what to validate next, not to decide on its own.
