### Assistant

**Astronomy interest on Ukrainian Wikipedia is declining, not growing.** Here's what the data shows:

## What was measured
**Astronomy** (Wikidata Q333: natural science studying celestial objects and phenomena) on the Ukrainian Wikipedia article "Астрономія," comparing September 2025–August 2026 with the same months a year earlier.

## Results for Ukrainian Wikipedia
"Article views fell 59.6% (16,614 → 6,708 views in total over the 12 compared months). The whole uk Wikipedia shrank 24.6% over the same time, so the article's share of all views fell 46.4%: it lost ground relative to the rest of that Wikipedia." The article averaged 559 views per month recently.

## Trust: Medium confidence, and not trustworthy for growth
**Verdict**: declining  
**Confidence**: medium  
**Why**: about 559 views a month is a small base; the change in share (−46.4%) is larger than the ±26% that month-to-month variation could produce; the trend holds for typical months, not just the spike in 2024-09; 10 of 12 months were below the same month a year earlier.

For an educational app decision, this is a **weak signal**—the decline is real enough to rule out as a growing market, but the small sample size (559 views/month) means the absolute numbers are fragile.

## Key caveat
Pageviews measure curiosity among Wikipedia readers, not willingness to pay or market size. Ukrainian reader interest falling doesn't automatically mean no market exists, just that Wikipedia curiosity isn't growing.
