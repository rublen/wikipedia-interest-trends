## How far to trust it

**Medium confidence is meaningful here.** The decline appears genuine:
- The share fell 46.4%, which is far larger than the ±26% month-to-month variation you'd expect from noise
- The trend holds across typical months, not just one spike (in 10 of 12 months, the share was lower than the same month a year earlier)
- However, the base is small (~559 views/month), which narrows the precision
