## How far to trust this
**Medium confidence.** The data supports the declining trend:
- The change in share (-46.4%) is larger than the ±26% that month-to-month variation could produce  
- 10 of 12 months were below the same month a year earlier
- But the monthly base (559 views) is modest, which limits precision

The main weakness is the small volume—fluctuations are more likely at this scale. However, the consistency (10 of 12 months down) and the magnitude of the decline both point to a real shift in reader interest.

## Caveats
