**What was measured**: Astronomy (the natural science studying celestial objects and phenomena; article "Астрономія" on uk.wikipedia.org), comparing September 2025–August 2026 against September 2024–August 2025.

**The trend**: "Article views fell 59.6% (16,614 → 6,708). The whole uk Wikipedia shrank 24.6% over the same time, so the article's share of all views fell 46.4%: it lost ground relative to the rest of that Wikipedia."

**How far to trust this?** **Medium confidence**. The reasons:
- About 559 views a month is a small base
- The change in share (−46.4%) is larger than the ±26% that month-to-month variation could produce
- The trend holds for typical months, not just a spike in 2024-09
- 10 of 12 months were below the same month a year earlier

Medium confidence means the signal is real enough to act on, but not airtight—don't bet the farm on it alone. The small baseline and seasonal noise leave some room for error, but the consistent 10-of-12 decline across most months is a fairly solid indicator.

**Key caveat**: Wikipedia pageviews measure curiosity among readers, not willingness to pay or actual market demand. A course in astronomy might still succeed in Ukraine; you'd want to combine this with user research, survey data, or signals from other markets.
