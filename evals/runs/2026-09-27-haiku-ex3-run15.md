## Report: English Learning Interest Across 8 Audiences

This serves as a proxy for reader interest in the English language itself, measured across eight Wikipedia language editions in their native languages. Compared: September 2025–August 2026 vs. September 2024–August 2025.

---

### Interest Trends by Audience

**Rank 1: German (de)** — 23,477 views/month. Article views fell 8.0% overall, but the German Wikipedia also shrank 7.3%, so the article's share moved 0.7%, within normal fluctuation: **no clear change, with medium confidence**. (Note: 75% of views in one period were bot traffic, which may inflate some months.)

**Rank 2: Vietnamese (vi)** — 12,478 views/month. Article views fell 23.1%, but the Vietnamese Wikipedia shrank 24.2%, so the article's share moved 1.6%, within normal fluctuation: **no clear change, with high confidence**.

**Rank 3–4: Turkish (tr) & Japanese (ja)**
- **Turkish:** 7,080 views/month. Share moved 0.2%, within fluctuation: **no clear change, high confidence**.
- **Japanese:** 36,383 views/month (largest audience). Share fell 11.0%: **declining, high confidence**. Notably, every single month was lower than a year prior.

**Rank 5–8: Spanish, Polish, Portuguese, Ukrainian** — All **declining** in share (10–15% drops). Ukrainian fell hardest (14.6% drop, smallest audience at 6,659 views/month). Spanish and Portuguese have **low confidence** (the declines came from a few unusual months), so validate before acting.

---

### **Recommendation: Which audiences to explore next**

**Explore first: German and Vietnamese.** These two stand out:

- **German** ranks #1: stable momentum, 3rd-largest audience (23.5k views/month), medium confidence.
- **Vietnamese** ranks #2: stable momentum, high confidence, growing absolute interest base.

Together, they represent audiences showing **sustained attention** to English—unlike the consistent declines in ja, uk, pl, es, and pt. Validate demand in these two before expanding to the declining markets.

**Lower priority on this evidence:** Japanese (largest raw audience but declining), Turkish, Spanish, Polish, Ukrainian, Portuguese. All show downward momentum.

---

### Notes & Confidence

**Trust the stable results (de, vi, tr)** — high or medium confidence; patterns were consistent across 12 months. **Be cautious with declines in es and pt** — the skill notes these came from a few unusual months, so check what happened during spikes before relying on them. 

**Scope:** These numbers measure reader interest on Wikipedia, not willingness to pay or addressable market size. Use them to choose what to validate next in user research, not to decide alone.
