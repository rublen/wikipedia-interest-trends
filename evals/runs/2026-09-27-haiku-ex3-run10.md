### **Which audiences to explore next—and why:**

**Explore next: German and Vietnamese.**

**Why not the others:** Japanese has the largest audience but is actively declining. Polish and Spanish are mid-sized but both declining. Turkish, Portuguese, and Ukrainian are smaller and declining or unstable.
